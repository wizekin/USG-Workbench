# Phase 4 — Step 4.8: Assertion and Evidence Construction

**Status:** IMPLEMENTED — pending exact-PR CI and merge acceptance  
**Adapter:** `assertion-evidence-adapter/0.1.0`

The adapter projects a unique, resolved semantic composition into native USG-SEM/1.0-shaped assertion and evidence records. It deliberately uses assertion lifecycle `DISCOVERED`, epistemic status `UNKNOWN`, commitment `NONE`, and evaluation context `UNKNOWN`. Exact quotation alignment is recorded as `EXACT`, while semantic support remains `NOT_ESTABLISHED`.

No author commitment, entailment, corroboration, verification, or truth is inferred from the mere presence of a grammatical sentence. Ambiguous, unresolved, unsupported, or malformed compositions produce no assertion/evidence projection. The full canonical document assembly and validation remain Step 4.11.
