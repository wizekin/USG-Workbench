# USG Milestone 1 — Status after v0.5.1

**Status: Not yet certified complete.**

## What v0.5.1 now implements

- Canonical `USG-SR/0.5.1` semantic graph: `PREDICATION`, `OPERATOR`, and `CONNECTIVE` expressions.
- Explicit nested negation and modality; `EXPECTED` is distinct from `MUST`.
- Correct structural distinction between `NOT(FORALL ...)` and `FORALL(... NOT(...))` for supported `not every` and `no` patterns.
- A source-grounded composer that parses clauses independently and builds selected conditional, exception, temporal, concessive, causal, contrastive, disjunctive, conjunctive, and discourse structures.
- Clause-local tense and limited source-grounded coreference.
- Source-grounded event frequency attributes.
- Empty quantifier metadata and placeholder/unmatched event attributes are discarded rather than promoted into false semantic content.
- The source composer only claims a complete parse when all clauses in its supported input pattern and required arguments are represented.
- When the composer is used, the original LLM proposal and its warnings remain in `modelProposalSummary` for audit.
- Human review, provenance, evidence, and objective-truth boundaries remain.

## Composition coverage and scope

The composer can process the fixed 14-sentence benchmark and additional lexical/order variations in deterministic tests. It supports one principal connective construction per source input and short multi-sentence discourse. It is not a full parser: arbitrary nested connectives, reported speech, relative clauses, complex coordination, broad passive constructions, unrestricted coreference, and unrestricted verb valency are not yet supported. When a source cannot be safely covered, the composer returns no parse and the ordinary model-proposal path remains in use.

## Remaining acceptance work

1. Run the same 14 sentences on Qwen and Gemma locally in v0.5.1.
2. Inspect raw model responses, `modelProposalSummary`, normalized claims, `semanticModel`, and `semanticValidation` separately.
3. Test unsupported/unseen forms, ambiguity, multi-connective nesting, passive voice, reported speech, and valency variation.
4. Create an independently human-labelled unseen-sentence benchmark.
5. Measure clause segmentation, predicate/argument accuracy, polarity/scope, modality, tense, quantification, connective relations, coreference, and unsupported/unresolved handling separately.

Milestone 1 should not be certified until these accuracy tests show reliable results and important failures are corrected.
